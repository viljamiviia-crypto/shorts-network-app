Privacy policy

Shorts Network · Last updated 2 November 2026

Who this applies to

Shorts Network is a private tool its owner uses for their own YouTube channels. This policy explains what happens to data when a YouTube channel is connected to it.

YouTube API Services

Shorts Network uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What data it accesses

Only with the permission granted through Google sign-in, Shorts Network:

  • uploads videos that the owner chooses, with their titles, descriptions and tags;
  • reads the channel's ID, title, list of uploads and each video's public counts (views, likes, comments);
  • reads YouTube Analytics reports for the channel's videos: views, watch time, average percentage viewed, audience retention, traffic sources, shares and subscribers gained.

It does not access other Google data, such as email, contacts or files.

How the data is used

Only to publish the owner's videos and to report how they perform, so future videos can be improved. The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Where it is stored

Sign-in tokens and performance reports are stored only on the owner's own computer. Shorts Network has no server of its own, and this website uses no cookies or tracking.

Keeping and deleting data

Sign-in tokens are kept until access is revoked. You can revoke Shorts Network's access at any time in your Google Account's third-party connections page; the stored token then stops working. To have a stored token or reports deleted, email the address below.
